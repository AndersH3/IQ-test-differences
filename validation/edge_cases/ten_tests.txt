INTERPRETATION
mc_se and mc_95_* quantify simulation error, not uncertainty in rho or
the assumptions, and not the spread of an individual person's scores.
squared_difference has units of IQ points squared; other means use IQ points.
The 10 pairwise gaps within a row of 5 tests are not independent replicates.
We use one specified pair and one range per independent person.
The simulation supports implementation/numerics; the mathematical derivation
proves the expectations under the stated model.
